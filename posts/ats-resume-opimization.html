How to Optimize Your Resume for ATS

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes. Here’s how to make yours pass.

1. Use Keywords from Job Description

Scan the job advert and include relevant terms.

2. Simple Formatting

Avoid tables, images, and complex layouts.

3. Standard Section Headings

Use headings like Work Experience, Education, Skills.

4. Submit in PDF or Word

Check the company’s preference.

5. Test Your Resume

Use our ATS Resume Scanner tool to see your match percentage.
