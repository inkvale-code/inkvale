How to Start an Anonymous Side Hustle

Step-by-step plan for creating income online without showing your face or building a public brand.

  1. Pick a micro-niche problem to solve
  2. Create a small guide, PDF, or tool
  3. Publish 3–5 short articles
  4. Sell your first product (£5–£25)
  5. Collect feedback and iterate
